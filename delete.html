Hajira Khata – Delete your account and data

App: Hajira Khata (package: com.mobiletips.hajirakhata), developer: Hajira Khata.

Option 1 – Inside the app

1. Open Hajira Khata.
2. Open the menu (☰) and tap Backup & sign in.
3. Tap Delete account & cloud records and confirm.

Your Google sign-in link and all records saved in the cloud are deleted immediately.

Option 2 – By email

Send an email to hajirakhatainfo@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Hajira Khata account". We will delete your account and cloud data within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account (email and Google sign-in ID) and all records backed up to the cloud (work days, overtime, bazar, bills, payments and settings). Nothing is kept after deletion.

Records stored only on your phone are not affected. You can remove them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
