Hajira Khata – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Developer: Hajira Khata (RASEL MIA) · Package: com.mobiletips.hajirakhata

Your data

All your overtime, night bill, bazar, loan and expense records are stored only on your phone. We do not collect, send, view or sell them. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to stay free. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from the IP address, and general device information to show ads and measure their performance. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Notifications

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to send app notices. None of your records are sent.

Children

This app is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all your records from your phone.
