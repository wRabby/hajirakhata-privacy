Hajira Khata – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Developer: Hajira Khata (RASEL MIA) · Package: com.mobiletips.hajirakhata

Your data

All your overtime, night bill, bazar, loan and expense records are kept on your phone. We never sell them.

Backup and sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect your name and email address, and a copy of your records is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so you can restore them on another phone. Only you can access your records. Data is encrypted in transit. Sign-in is optional; the app works fully without it.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to stay free. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from the IP address, and general device information to show ads and measure their performance. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Notifications

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to send app notices.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the menu → "Backup & sign in" → "Delete account & cloud records". This permanently deletes your account and all cloud records. You can also request deletion here: https://wrabby.github.io/hajirakhata-privacy/delete.html

Children

This app is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
